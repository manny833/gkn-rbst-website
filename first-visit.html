YOUR FIRST VISIT

A warm welcome
starts here

If this is your first time visiting a Gurdwara, there's nothing to worry about. A few simple customs help everyone share the space respectfully.

01

Cover your head

Everyone covers their head in the Darbar Sahib. Ask on arrival if you need guidance.

02

Remove your shoes

Leave shoes in the designated area before entering the prayer hall.

03

Enter respectfully

Move quietly, follow guidance from sevadars and keep phones away in the prayer hall.

04

Check before travelling

Call the Gurdwara to confirm current opening and programme times.
