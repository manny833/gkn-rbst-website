Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji Gurpurab & Nishan Sahib Seva

The sangat of Guru Ka Niwas marks Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s Parkash Gurpurab on 24 November 2026 with the washing and cleaning of the Nishan Sahib. A day of devotion, unity and selfless service, in photographs.

Tuesday 24 November 2026Nishan Sahib Seva81 photographs
01The sangat comes together

Preparing for seva

Members of the sangat gathered at Guru Ka Niwas to take part in the Nishan Sahib Seva.

02Ishnaan seva

Washing the Nishan Sahib

The Nishan Sahib was washed and cleaned in preparation for another year. The photographs capture the sangat working together in the spirit of seva.

03Chola Sahib

Preparing the covering

Sevadars worked together with the orange Chola Sahib, continuing the care of the Nishan Sahib.

04The Nishan Sahib

Standing tall

The photographs capture the closing stages of the seva and the Nishan Sahib being raised.

With gratitude

Happy Gurpurab to all the sangat

May the teachings of Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji — Naam Japna, Kirat Karni and Vand Chhakna — continue to inspire remembrance of the Divine, honest living, sharing with others and selfless service.

Thank you to everyone who gave their seva. Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.
